Who We Are

THE NYLON
SPECIALISTS

Rash_Poly is Ogun State's leading nylon company, proudly located at Orita Money, Ilogbo Oju-Ore, Otta. We specialize in the buying and selling of premium-quality nylon products — from industrial packaging to everyday consumer bags.

Built on trust, quality, and community, we supply businesses, markets, factories, and individuals with nylons that meet the highest standards. Whether you're purchasing in bulk wholesale or selling raw nylon materials, Rash_Poly is your most reliable partner.

What We Do

OUR CORE
SERVICES

From wholesale to retail, buying to supply — we cover every angle of the nylon trade.

01 🛍️

Nylon Selling

We offer a wide range of nylon bags, packaging materials, and industrial films. Available in all sizes, thicknesses, and quantities — from small retail to large-scale wholesale orders.

02 🔄

Nylon Buying

We purchase raw, scrap, and used nylon materials at the best market rates. Bring your nylon materials to us — we offer fair pricing and prompt payment for all grades and types.

03 🚚

Bulk Supply

We supply nylon products in bulk to businesses, markets, factories and institutions across Ogun State and beyond. Large-volume orders welcome with competitive pricing.
